##Features of Cinema

- Small theater with 8 rows and 10 seats per row.
- Customer often try to buy seats together, so staff need to know which seats are available and which are reserved.
- Cinema room has two entrance/exit, 1 at each side of the room.
- The screen is at the bottom, it means the further row from the screen is row "A"
